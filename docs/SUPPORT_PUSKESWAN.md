# Support Puskeswan | Product and Technical Specification v0.1

## Goal
Connect existing APEL service reports and Puskeswan profiles to a defensible, traceable workflow for **identifying, verifying, providing and evaluating provincial support**. This is a planning/assistance tool, not an independent public procurement or budget approval system.

## Users and permissions
- **Puskeswan reporter:** submit needs, upload evidence, track own requests, acknowledge receipt.
- **Kabupaten/kota coordinator:** review submissions within jurisdiction, provide context/verification.
- **Provincial verifier:** verify needs, prioritize and recommend support.
- **Provincial admin:** manage catalogs and records within assigned authority.
- **Leadership:** read-only aggregated dashboard and approval status if authorized.

## Workflow
1. Need identified from profile, field observation, or Puskeswan request.
2. Submission lists category, quantity, justification, urgency, period, and evidence.
3. Verification checks completeness, current inventory, practical need and availability of alternatives.
4. Prioritization combines objective indicators with transparent explanations and opportunity for manual correction.
5. Formal authorized decision and available budget are recorded, not automatically generated by scoring.
6. Support package planned, distributed and acknowledged (BAST / handover evidence).
7. Utilization and effectiveness reviewed after distribution.

## Need categories
- Basic clinical tools and maintenance
- Cold-chain equipment (coolbox, ice pack)
- Medicines and consumables (obat/BHP), subject to applicable restrictions
- Emergency operational support where specifically authorized
- Other documented provincial support

## Proposed data objects
- `puskeswan`: stable ID, kab/kota, organization, profile metadata.
- `support_need`: puskeswan_id, category, requested_item, quantity, condition, justification, urgency, evidence reference, created_by, created_at.
- `need_verification`: need_id, verifier, finding, status, reason, verified_at.
- `priority_assessment`: need_id, indicator values, weights version, score, rationale, human override reason.
- `support_proposal`: verified need(s), program/funding reference (no automatic commitment), approval status and responsible actor.
- `support_delivery`: proposal_id, items, quantities, deliverer, received_by, BAST reference, dates.
- `support_followup`: delivery_id, utilization notes, condition, next action and follow-up date.
- `audit_event`: actor, action, object ID, timestamp, before/after metadata (no secrets).

## Suggested priority indicators (weights not final)
- Facility readiness gap (essential equipment/nonfunctional assets)
- Service need and case mix from APEL, normalized by staffing/catchment
- Geographic access difficulty or disaster impact
- Risk to animal health/public service continuity
- Verified stock shortage and feasibility of support

**Fairness rule:** low service volume by itself is **not** grounds for low priority. Final allocation is human-approved and constrained by legal authority and available budget.

## Interfaces to prototype
1. Puskeswan: 'Kebutuhan Saya' submission/history.
2. Kabupaten/Kota: area verification inbox.
3. Provinsi: verified needs + ranked view with explainable rationale.
4. Admin: support package, approvals, delivery and BAST log.
5. Leadership: aggregate needs, pending actions and realization.
